# Agent instructions

Context repo for Jahlon's work at the UC Berkeley Neurotech Hackathon (October 24 to 25, 2026, Openwater sponsor). The team is building on Open-Motion with the station available all weekend. Read README.md, then docs/OPEN-MOTION.md, docs/EVENT.md, and docs/TRACKS.md before proposing a project.

Event scope rules apply to anything built from this context:

- Work from provided datasets, open-source repositories, simulation, and software. Physical devices are shared stations in scheduled slots.
- Openwater tracks do not place a device on a person or collect human data. Do not plan around new human recordings for an Openwater track.
- Outputs are research engineering demonstrations. Make no diagnostic, screening, clinical, treatment, or therapy claims. Say "for research."
- No modification of safety-critical optical or acoustic output.

The source docs in sources/ are snapshots. The Google Docs are live; re-read them before relying on device lists, slots, or track wording. Keep source URL, owner, and capture time with any new snapshot.

No em dashes, slogans, or ornamental taglines. Keep plans, drafts, built work, and verified results distinct.
