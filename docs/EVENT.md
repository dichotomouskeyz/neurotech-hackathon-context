# Event

**UC Berkeley Neurotech Hackathon**, October 24 to 25, 2026. Openwater is the sponsor and runs the challenge tracks. Openwater's opening pitch is given by Dan Blizinski. Judging is Sunday afternoon.

## Prompt

How can neurotechnology expand human cognitive potential?

## Scope rules

From the Openwater tracks document:

- Every track can be completed with provided datasets, open-source repositories, simulation, and software.
- Physical devices are shared stations available in scheduled slots. Teams book slots with Openwater engineers at the stations.
- No track involves placing a device on a person or collecting human data.
- Outputs are research engineering demonstrations, not diagnostic, screening, or clinical-decision tools.
- Teams pick one track or propose their own under Wildcard. Tracks are suggestions, not specs. The goal is a working demonstration by Sunday afternoon.
