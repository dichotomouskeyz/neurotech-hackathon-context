# Hackathon context

Notes from the Neurotech@Berkeley hackathon, October 24 to 25, 2026, sponsored by Openwater. The product is this repository. Start at the [README](../README.md).

[OPEN-MOTION.md](hackathon/OPEN-MOTION.md) records that Openwater approved self-testing by team members for this project. [EVENT.md](hackathon/EVENT.md) keeps the earlier statement of work, which said no human data. The Google Docs behind `sources/` were live during the weekend. Re-read them before relying on device lists or track details.
