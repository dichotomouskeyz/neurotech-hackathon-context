# Openwater

Summary of the opening pitch deck (six slides with speaker notes).

## Platforms

- **Open-LIFU:** low-intensity focused ultrasound for targeted neuromodulation research. The station demo is a water tank with a 64-element transducer steered by open code.
- **Open-Motion:** real-time cerebral blood flow monitoring (BFI, BVI).

Both are open source from hardware through software.

## Positioning

- One programmable, software-defined platform across many applications instead of one device per disease.
- Lower-cost hardware for broader access.
- Shared infrastructure for institutions and companies.
- Deck lists research users for Open-LIFU (Stanford, Duke, Mayo, MIT Lincoln Lab, University of Glasgow on human ability augmentation, others) and Open-Motion (Penn, Emory, MGB, UC Irvine, St. Jude, others).

## Language

Speaker notes tell the presenter to say "for research" and never "treat" or "therapy," and not to name competing modalities. The deck carries a disclaimer that the system is not FDA evaluated and not designed for diagnosis or treatment. Project write-ups and demos should follow the same framing.

## What they ask of teams

1. Visit the stations, meet engineers, book device slots. Every track works from datasets and simulation without a slot.
2. Read docs.openwater.health.
3. Fork github.com/OpenwaterHealth. Pull requests from the weekend are welcome.
4. Join the Discord for help afterward.
