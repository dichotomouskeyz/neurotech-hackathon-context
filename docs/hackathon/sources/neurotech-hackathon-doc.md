## prompt

**How can neurotechnology expand human cognitive potential?**

- Physical devices are shared stations available in scheduled slots.
- No track involves placing a device on a person or collecting human data.
- Outputs are research engineering demonstrations, not diagnostic, screening, or clinical-decision tools.

| Track | Platform | Example directions | Typical skills |
| :- | :- | :- | :- |
| Signal & Physiology | Open-Motion | Pulse and heart rate, respiration, HRV, artifact rejection, left-right asymmetry | DSP, Python… |
| Real-Time Viz & Interface | Open-Motion | Dashboards, laterality views, annotation, replay | UI/UX, viz… |
| ML & Brain-State | Open-Motion | Cognitive-load or task classification on provided data, leakage-free evaluation | ML, stats… |
| Acoustic Simulation & Planning | Open-LIFU | Beam steering and pressure-field simulation, target workflows, SlicerOpenLIFU UX | Acoustics, numerical, Qt |
| Imaging & Registration | Open-LIFU | MRI tissue segmentation, landmark detection, surface-registration error metrics | Medical imaging, 3D geometry |
| Hardware & Wearables | Both | Headset fit and coupling, fixtures, firmware or FPGA study on the bench | ME, EE, firmware |
| Wildcard | Any | Anything creative that fits none of the above | Any |
